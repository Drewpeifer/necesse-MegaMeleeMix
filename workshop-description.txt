[h1]Mega Melee Mix[/h1]
A little something for the melee crowd: four melee weapons and a tanky trinket. Updated for Necesse 1.3.3.

[h2]Weapons[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Exsanguinator[/b] (sword): 50% chance to heal for 30% of your armor on hit
[*][b]The Cauterizer[/b] (greatsword): sets the target ablaze for 10 seconds, burning for 50% of your current health each second. More health means more damage, so tanks hit hardest.
[*][b]The Tranquilizer[/b] (hammer): paralyzes the target for 3 seconds and delivers huge knockback
[*][b]The Boomy Whacker[/b] (spear): creates an explosion on EVERY swing at your cursor, scaling with weapon damage. Don't use indoors!
[/list]

[h2]Trinket[/h2]
[b]Warlord's Pauldron[/b]
[list]
[*]+50% Resilience Gain
[*]+100 Max Resilience
[*]+100 Max Health
[*]+25% Health Regen
[*]+1 Max Food Buffs
[*]-15% Attack Speed
[*]-15% Crit Chance
[/list]

[h2]Recipes[/h2]
All crafted at the Iron Anvil.
[list]
[*]The Exsanguinator: 15 Tungsten Bar
[*]The Cauterizer: 35 Tungsten Bar
[*]The Tranquilizer: 25 Mycelium Bar
[*]The Boomy Whacker: 20 each of any log, Copper, Iron, Gold and Tungsten Bar, plus 1 Dynamite Stick
[*]Warlord's Pauldron: 5 Challenger's Pauldron
[/list]

The Boomy Whacker and Warlord's Pauldron can also turn up in loot.
